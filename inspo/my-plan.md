## Basic training rules

- Warm up for 5–10 minutes before lifting.
- During your first 2–3 rotations, perform only **two working sets per exercise**.
- Choose weights that leave approximately **2–3 good repetitions in reserve**.
- Rest 1–2 minutes between isolation exercises and 2–3 minutes after large presses, rows, hack squats and leg presses.
- Once you reach the highest listed repetition count on every set with good form, increase the weight by one small increment.
- Start with 15–20 minutes of cardio and gradually work toward 30 minutes.
- Stop if you experience sharp or joint pain; muscular effort and mild fatigue are normal.
- Have gym staff show you the correct seat and lever adjustments for each machine.
